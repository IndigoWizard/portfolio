About Me

I'm a Geospatial Developer and Environmental Analyst with expertise in geospatial analysis, remote sensing and environmental studies. I combine technical skills with domain knowledge to develop practical solutions that help assess environmental impacts and understand changes across urban and natural environments.

My work spans from building interactive geospatial applications to performing spatial analysis and developing automated workflows using Python and open-source technologies. Through satellite imagery, environmental data, and geospatial tools, I aim to transform complex datasets into accessible insights that support research, environmental monitoring and informed decision-making.
